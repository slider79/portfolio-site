About me

I'm Muhammad Shuja Jamal, a software engineering student at FAST NUCES Lahore, currently interning across AI, networking and security at once.

I build across the stack: React and Next.js on the front, Node and Django behind it, and increasingly Python where the interesting problems are. Lately that has meant AI agents that call tools, retrieval systems that stay honest about their sources, and a peer-to-peer network with no server in the middle of it. I like turning messy problems into clean, well-documented software, and I would rather test a tool myself than read what its landing page claims.

Off the keyboard: photography, running, and motorcycles.
